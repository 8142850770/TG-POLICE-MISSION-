TS SI / PC Syllabus Guide

Use this as a preparation checklist. Confirm exact topics and marks from the latest official TSLPRB notification.

📚 Arithmetic & Reasoning
  • Number system, simplification, HCF & LCM
  • Percentages, ratio & proportion, averages
  • Profit & loss, discount, simple and compound interest
  • Time & work, pipes & cisterns, time-speed-distance
  • Data interpretation, mensuration
  • Analogy, series, coding-decoding, directions, blood relations
  • Syllogisms, ranking, calendars and clocks
🇮🇳 General Studies & Current Affairs
  • Indian history and national movement
  • Indian and Telangana geography
  • Indian Constitution and polity
  • Indian economy and basic economic concepts
  • General science: physics, chemistry, biology
  • Environmental issues, technology, awards, sports and important events
🏛️ Indian Polity
  • Constitutional history, Preamble and salient features
  • Fundamental Rights, DPSP and Fundamental Duties
  • President, Vice-President, Prime Minister and Council of Ministers
  • Parliament, Supreme Court, High Courts and judicial review
  • Constitutional bodies, amendments, emergency provisions
  • Centre–State relations, local government and elections
  • Telangana formation, state government and relevant state provisions
Revision tip: Learn Articles, Parts, Schedules, amendments and landmark cases in separate tables.
🗺️ Telangana History, Movement & Culture
  • Historical dynasties and important places
  • Telangana social, cultural and literary heritage
  • Telangana movement: key phases, events and personalities
  • State formation and administrative structure
  • Major irrigation projects, rivers, festivals and institutions
🔬 General Science
  • Physics: units, motion, force, work, energy, heat, light and electricity
  • Chemistry: matter, atoms, acids/bases/salts, metals and everyday chemistry
  • Biology: cells, human body, nutrition, diseases, plants and ecology
  • Science and technology in everyday life
📖 English / Language Skills
  • Grammar, vocabulary, synonyms and antonyms
  • Sentence correction, comprehension and usage
  • Practise Telugu or other language-paper topics only where required by the current notification
Important: SI and PC selection stages, paper pattern, qualifying rules and detailed syllabus may differ. This is a study checklist, not an official notification.

Quick Study Notes

Short exam-focused facts for rapid revision.

🏛️ Polity: Constitution basics
  • The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949.
  • It came into force on 26 January 1950.
  • The Drafting Committee was chaired by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar.
  • Fundamental Rights are in Part III (Articles 12–35).
  • DPSP are in Part IV (Articles 36–51).
  • Fundamental Duties are in Part IVA (Article 51A).
గుర్తుంచుకోండి: Adopted — 26 Nov 1949; Enforced — 26 Jan 1950.
📐 Arithmetic: Percentage
  • x% = x/100
  • Percentage = (part ÷ whole) × 100
  • 10% = 1/10; 25% = 1/4; 50% = 1/2; 75% = 3/4
  • If a value increases by 20%, multiply by 1.20. If it decreases by 20%, multiply by 0.80.
Example: 25% of 240 = 240 ÷ 4 = 60.
🔬 Physics: Units & Measurements
  • Length — metre (m)
  • Mass — kilogram (kg)
  • Time — second (s)
  • Electric current — ampere (A)
  • Temperature — kelvin (K)
  • Force — newton (N); work/energy — joule (J); power — watt (W)
Exam tip: Learn SI base quantities and common derived units together.
🗺️ Geography: Quick revision
  • Revise Indian states, capitals, rivers, dams and neighbouring countries.
  • For Telangana, revise major rivers, districts, irrigation projects, crops and climate.
  • Practise map-based recall instead of only rereading lists.
📜 History: Study method
  • Make a timeline for ancient, medieval and modern Indian history.
  • For the national movement, connect year + event + leader.
  • Revise Telangana history and movement as separate chronological timelines.
